# Enterprise Audit Feature Controls Guide & Practical Examples

This document explains the two primary enterprise audit feature control modules in the **Procurement Analytics & Risk Management System**, complete with mathematical formulas and step-by-step numerical examples.

---

## 1. Invoice Risk Flagging Audit Controls

The **Invoice Risk Flagging** system uses a hybrid architecture combining hard business rule thresholds with Random Forest machine learning classification. Finance auditors can dynamically adjust three core control parameters in the sidebar.

### Control 1.1: Max Dollar Discrepancy Tolerance ($)
- **Parameter:** `dollar_tolerance`
- **Sidebar Slider:** `Max Dollar Discrepancy ($)` (Range: $1.00 to $50.00, Default: $5.00)
- **Purpose:** Defines the maximum acceptable dollar difference between the billed invoice total and the purchase order (PO) line item total.
- **Evaluation Formula:**
  $$\text{Dollar Discrepancy} = |\text{Invoice Dollars} - \text{Total Item Dollars}|$$
  $$\text{Trigger Condition:} \quad \text{Dollar Discrepancy} > \text{dollar\_tolerance}$$

#### Numerical Example:
- **PO Line Item Expected Total:** $500.00
- **Vendor Billed Invoice Amount:** $508.00
- **Calculated Discrepancy:** $|508.00 - 500.00| = \$8.00$

- **Case A (Default Tolerance = $5.00):**
  - $\$8.00 > \$5.00 \rightarrow$ **FLAGGED for Manual Review**
  - **Risk Reason Tag:** `Price Discrepancy ($8.00 > $5.00)`
- **Case B (Relaxed Auditor Tolerance = $10.00):**
  - $\$8.00 \le \$10.00 \rightarrow$ **PASSED Rule** (No price discrepancy alert)

---

### Control 1.2: ML Anomaly Threshold (%)
- **Parameter:** `prob_cutoff`
- **Sidebar Slider:** `ML Anomaly Threshold (%)` (Range: 30% to 90%, Default: 50%)
- **Purpose:** Sets the minimum confidence probability generated by the Random Forest model required to trigger an ML anomaly flag.
- **Evaluation Formula:**
  $$\text{Trigger Condition:} \quad \text{ML Risk Probability} \ge \text{prob\_cutoff}$$

#### Numerical Example:
- **Invoice Input Data:** An invoice with high historical risk characteristics.
- **Random Forest ML Probability Output:** 65.0% confidence score.

- **Case A (Default Threshold = 50%):**
  - $65.0\% \ge 50.0\% \rightarrow$ **FLAGGED for Manual Review**
  - **Risk Reason Tag:** `ML Anomaly Score (65.0% >= 50.0%)`
- **Case B (Conservative Auditor Threshold = 70%):**
  - $65.0\% < 70.0\% \rightarrow$ **PASSED ML Threshold** (Score is below active cutoff)

---

### Control 1.3: Min PO Delay Threshold (Days)
- **Parameter:** `po_delay_threshold`
- **Sidebar Slider:** `Min PO Delay Threshold (Days)` (Range: 1 to 60 days, Default: 15 days)
- **Purpose:** Sets the maximum allowable elapsed days between the Purchase Order date and Invoice date before triggering a PO delay audit flag.
- **Evaluation Formula:**
  $$\text{Trigger Condition:} \quad \text{Days PO to Invoice} > \text{po\_delay\_threshold}$$

#### Numerical Example:
- **PO Created Date:** January 1
- **Invoice Issued Date:** January 21 (20 days delay)

- **Case A (Default Threshold = 15 days):**
  - $20 \text{ days} > 15 \text{ days} \rightarrow$ **FLAGGED for Manual Review**
  - **Risk Reason Tag:** `PO Delay (20 days > 15 days)`
- **Case B (Adjusted Threshold = 25 days):**
  - $20 \text{ days} \le 25 \text{ days} \rightarrow$ **PASSED Delay Rule** (Delay is within acceptable window)

## 2. Freight Overcharge Audit Controls

The **Freight Overcharge Audit** system evaluates actual shipping charges billed by vendors against an ML regression baseline.

### Control 2.1: Freight Overcharge Threshold (%)
- **Parameter:** `freight_tolerance_pct`
- **Sidebar Slider:** `Freight Overcharge Threshold (%)` (Range: 5.0% to 50.0%, Default: 15.0%)
- **Purpose:** Sets the percentage threshold above the expected ML freight baseline that triggers an `OVERCHARGED` audit status.
- **Evaluation Formulas:**
  $$\text{Predicted Freight} = \text{ML Baseline Cost (\$)}$$
  $$\text{Freight Variance (\$)} = \text{Actual Billed Freight} - \text{Predicted Freight}$$
  $$\text{Variance \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Freight Variance}}{\text{Predicted Freight}} \right) \times 100$$
  $$\text{Trigger Condition:} \quad \text{Freight Variance} > \$5.00 \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{Variance \%} > \text{freight\_tolerance\_pct}$$

#### Numerical Example:
- **Invoice Total Value:** $1,850.00
- **ML Baseline Expected Freight:** $14.29
- **Actual Billed Freight on Invoice:** $25.00
- **Calculated Dollar Variance:** $\$25.00 - \$14.29 = +\$10.71$
- **Calculated Percentage Variance:** $\left( \frac{10.71}{14.29} \right) \times 100 = +74.9\%$

- **Case A (Default Threshold = 15.0%):**
  - $+\$10.71 > \$5.00$ AND $+74.9\% > 15.0\% \rightarrow$ **OVERCHARGED Alert**
  - **Audit Status:** `OVERCHARGED`
  - **Warning Message:** Billed freight ($25.00) is $10.71 (+74.9%) higher than ML baseline ($14.29).
- **Case B (High Tolerance Threshold = 80.0%):**
  - $+74.9\% \le 80.0\% \rightarrow$ **NORMAL**
  - **Audit Status:** `NORMAL`

## Summary of Audit Control Parameter Mapping

| Audit Module | Control Name | Variable Name | Default Value | Range | Impact on Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice Risk | Max Dollar Discrepancy | `dollar_tolerance` | $5.00 | $1.00 - $50.00 | Flags invoices where invoice vs PO total differs by > threshold |
| Invoice Risk | ML Anomaly Threshold | `prob_cutoff` | 50% | 30% - 90% | Flags invoices where ML model confidence score >= threshold |
| Invoice Risk | Min PO Delay Threshold | `po_delay_threshold` | 15 days | 1 - 60 days | Flags invoices issued > threshold days after PO creation |
| Freight Audit | Freight Overcharge Threshold | `freight_tolerance_pct` | 15.0% | 5.0% - 50.0% | Marks freight as OVERCHARGED when billed shipping > threshold % over ML baseline |
